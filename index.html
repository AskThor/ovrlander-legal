Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Ovrlander is a recovery technique reference app for overlanders. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the app:

  • Account information (email address) if you create an account or subscribe
  • Purchase and subscription data processed through Apple's App Store
  • App usage data to improve the experience (via anonymized analytics)
  • Rig profile and kit information you enter — stored locally on your device
  • Trip plans you generate and save — stored locally on your device

AI Trip Planner

Pro subscribers can use the AI Trip Planner. When you generate a trip plan, your inputs (terrain, rig details, kit list) are sent to our API to generate a response. We do not store or sell this data. The feature requires an internet connection and is intended for use before you leave for the trail.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To provide and improve app features
  • To respond to support requests
  • To send important account or app updates (not marketing, unless you opt in)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Apple — to process payments and manage subscriptions
  • RevenueCat — to manage subscription state
  • Anthropic — to power the AI Trip Planner (Pro tier only, inputs only, not stored)

Data Retention

Rig profiles, kit checklists, bookmarks, and saved trip plans are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this data. We retain account and subscription records only as long as necessary to provide service and meet legal obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. California residents have additional rights under CCPA. EU residents have rights under GDPR.

Children's Privacy

Ovrlander is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Disclaimer

All content in Ovrlander is for educational purposes only. Always exercise appropriate judgment in real-world situations. The app does not replace professional training or the advice of experienced overlanders.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
